Storytelling is my life, and I've looked for ways to tell stories since I can remember.

That instinct is what pulled me toward level design — it's the discipline where narrative, systems, and space all have to agree with each other. Whether it's a solo blockout or a team project built around an accessibility-first control scheme, I care about whether a space makes the player feel the thing it's supposed to make them feel.
